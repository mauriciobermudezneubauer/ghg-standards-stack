Energy Intensive Industry Product-Level GHG Measurement

The standards stack for carbon intensity of energies and products

There is no single product-level GHG standard for the energy and emissions intensive industries — there is a stack. Horizontal product carbon footprint grammar at the base, an industry-specific estimation substrate above it, the fast-moving product-attribute layer where the commercial action sits, and, at the top, the forcing functions — not regulation alone, but disclosure, commitments, capital markets and buyer demand — that turn voluntary measurement into market-access requirements, and increasingly into priced, tradable markets.

Each component's card carries an instrument type and three chips: maturity (how settled the method is), force (how much legal or commercial weight it carries) and adoption (who uses it). The one-line headline says what the component is; open the card for the facts, what comes next, the dated developments and any caveats. Every card links to its authoritative sources — verify there before quoting.
