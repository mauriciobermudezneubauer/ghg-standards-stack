Version history

What changed in each release of the standards stack. Corrections to earlier versions are stated as such. Current version v2.3, compiled as of 2026-11-12.

v2.3
2026-11-12
Monthly verification. All three EU institutions now back postponing the Methane Regulation import duties (one year or three). Five CBAM verifiers are accredited. The ETS reserve keeps invalidation at a 700 Mt threshold. Two cards added: the Renewable Energy Directive, previously referenced only through other cards, and Abu Dhabi facility emissions verification.
  • Added: EU Renewable Energy Directive (RED III); Abu Dhabi facility GHG MRV (Resolution No. 3 of 2026). Watch list: ISO 14060.
  • Updated: EU Methane Regulation, EU ETS, CBAM, UK CBAM, Article 6, pricing beyond the EU, GHG Protocol Scope 2, ISO × GHG Protocol, Carbon Measures, OGMP 2.0, energy attribute certificates, EnergyTag, Verra, ICVCM, buyer coalitions, hyperscaler procurement, WCI, ReFuelEU, sustainable finance, Global Carbon Council, Empowering Consumers Directive.
  • Corrected: OGMP 2.0 coverage (163 members, 43% of production; previously ~160 and ~45%); Heidelberg Edmonton project cost (CAD 1.36 bn estimate, up to about 2 bn; previously CAD 2 bn).
  • Omission stated: the Renewable Energy Directive should have been carded from v1; its Article 29 thresholds remain to be verified at the Official Journal text.
v2.2
2026-10-02
Monthly verification: the Commission announced work on a one-year postponement of the EU Methane Regulation's import provisions; the Methane Transparency Database missed its September target; a WTO panel was established on Russia's CBAM challenge; ResponsibleSteel opened consultation on Version 3.0.
  • Updated: EU Methane Regulation (postponement announced 29 Sep, not yet proposed; Transparency Database not launched), CBAM (WTO panel DS639, 25 Sep), ResponsibleSteel (V3.0 consultation 30 Sep–23 Nov), ASI (second consultation early 2027).
  • Verified, no change: IMO Net-Zero Framework (vote 4 Dec 2026), ISO 19870 parts at final draft, ISO 27920, OGMP 2.0, EU ETS maritime, FuelEU, ReFuelEU, CORSIA.
v2.1
2026-10-01
Monthly verification: CBAM default values corrected (Impl. Reg. 2026/1740), first CBAM verifier accreditation, accreditation instrument cited correctly, UK CBAM verification rules; compliance markets in Japan, India, Vietnam, China; new card for the EU Empowering Consumers Directive.
  • Added: EU Empowering Consumers Directive (claims rules, applies from 27 Sep 2026); watch-list entries for Puro.earth's Environmental Attributes Registry and the EFI Foundation ledger-based accounting draft.
  • Corrected: CBAM verifier accreditation is governed by Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2551, not Implementing Regulation 2025/2546 (which sets verification principles); default values corrected by Implementing Regulation 2026/1740 with effect from 1 January 2026.
  • Updated: CBAM (first verifier accreditation; third-country route; other-countries default), UK CBAM (SI 2026/995; qualifying-scheme list), carbon pricing beyond the EU (Japan, India, Vietnam, China, Mexico), market infrastructure (Xpansiv–Verdane; Equinor–Eneco on Attributes), Verra (Scope 3 Standard), WCI (California SB 1018 findings), Carbon Measures (advisory group), GHG Protocol Scope 2 (AMI RFI).
v2.0
2026-09-08
New card format and a full review of every component against primary sources.
  • Format: structured cards (type, touch points, links, how it works, key facts, developments, next steps, caveats, sources); maturity and force shown separately; search and filters; simple and detailed stack map; this version history.
  • Added: WCI (California · Québec · Washington) and IMO CII/EEXI; ISO 6338 series for the LNG chain (earlier versions stated no ISO LNG standard existed).
  • Corrected: CORSIA baseline is 85% of 2019 emissions; the CBAM–Article 6 link restated per the May 2026 draft implementing rules; ISO 19870 parts 2–4 shown at final draft; IMO Net-Zero Framework date shown as planned, subject to MEPC 85; GCCA rating bands corrected.
  • Updated: California LCFS 2024 amendments; US 45Q credit values and the July 2025 amendments; US federal Buy Clean rescission; CSRD scope under Directive (EU) 2026/470; IFRS S2 amendments of December 2025; GHG Protocol Scope 2 consultation results; worldsteel 2024 LCI release.
  • Sources replaced on 22 components; watch list and pull map rewritten in the same register as the cards.
