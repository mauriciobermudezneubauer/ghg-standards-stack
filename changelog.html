Version history

What changed in each release of the standards stack. Corrections to earlier versions are stated as such. Current version v2.0, compiled as of 2026-09-08.

v2.0
2026-09-08
New card format and a full review of every component against primary sources.
  • Format: structured cards (type, touch points, links, how it works, key facts, developments, next steps, caveats, sources); maturity and force shown separately; search and filters; simple and detailed stack map; this version history.
  • Added: WCI (California · Québec · Washington) and IMO CII/EEXI; ISO 6338 series for the LNG chain (earlier versions stated no ISO LNG standard existed).
  • Corrected: CORSIA baseline is 85% of 2019 emissions; the CBAM–Article 6 link restated per the May 2026 draft implementing rules; ISO 19870 parts 2–4 shown at final draft; IMO Net-Zero Framework date shown as planned, subject to MEPC 85; GCCA rating bands corrected.
  • Updated: California LCFS 2024 amendments; US 45Q credit values and the July 2025 amendments; US federal Buy Clean rescission; CSRD scope under Directive (EU) 2026/470; IFRS S2 amendments of December 2025; GHG Protocol Scope 2 consultation results; worldsteel 2024 LCI release.
  • Sources replaced on 22 components; watch list and pull map rewritten in the same register as the cards.
